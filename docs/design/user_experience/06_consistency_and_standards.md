# 06 - Consistency and Standards

Inconsistent patterns force users to relearn the product on every screen.

## 1. Familiarity (Jakob’s Law)

Users spend most of their time in other products, so they expect familiar patterns. Do not reinvent a control for novelty—for example, do not move a familiar back action to an unexpected side or replace a standard settings icon without a strong reason.

## 2. Internal Consistency

- **Action placement:** choose a consistent order for primary and secondary actions and use it across the product.
- **Copy:** use one term for one action. If the product says “Save”, do not switch to “Store” or “Keep” elsewhere for the same operation.
- **Design tokens:** source radius, shadows, and colors from shared CSS variables. Do not hard-code a page-specific variant.
